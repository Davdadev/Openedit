# Structures for short creative pieces

Pick one before choosing shots. Times are for a 15 s spot; scale for 6 s or 30 s.

## Hook → build → payoff → end card (the default for ads)

| Section | Time | Job | Shots | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–2 s | Stop the scroll: the most striking image, already moving | 1 hero | 1.5–2 s, a slow push-in |
| Build | 2–9 s | Variations on the idea, rising energy | 5–9 | 1.2 s → 0.5 s, quickening |
| Payoff | 9–12.5 s | The one moment the piece is about | 1–2 | slow into it, hold, a sound |
| End card | 12.5–15 s | Brand + one line | graphic over picture | 2–3 s, music resolves |

## Problem → solution (product, app, service)

Frustration (2–4 s, slightly desaturated or handheld) → the product appears (a hard cut, a
colour shift, the music kicks in) → it in use, fast (3–5 shots) → the result, held → end card.

## Rule of three (lifestyle, brand)

Three short vignettes with the same structure and a shared visual rhyme (same move, same
framing, same colour accent), the third twisting it; then the line. The repetition is the
rhythm; the twist is the payoff.

## Countdown / list (social)

Numbered beats, each 1.5–2.5 s with one kinetic word; fast cuts inside a beat, a hard reset
(flash or whip) between beats. Keep the count visible and small.

## Mood film (travel, event, fashion)

No hard story: open wide and quiet, tighten and quicken through the middle, end on one
held, human moment. Music drives it; cut to phrases (openedit-rhythm, `phrase` style), with
natural sound breaking through at two or three moments (`natSoundDb`).

## Trailer / teaser

Cold open (one intriguing shot + sound) → title flash → montage that accelerates to a peak →
a hard cut to silence → one last beat (the joke, the reveal) → title and date.

## Choosing shots for a section

- Hook: the sharpest, most colourful, most unusual frame; motion already happening.
- Build: variety in colour and setting, consistency in framing or motion (a rhyme).
- Payoff: an action completing (a bite, a catch, a splash, a reveal) — not a pretty still.
- End card background: something calm and on-brand (bokeh, a clean surface, the product
  resting), slowed down (`speed 0.5`) so the type reads.
