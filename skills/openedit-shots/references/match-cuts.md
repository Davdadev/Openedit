# Match cuts

Kinds find_match_cuts reports:
- **graphic**: same shape in the same place (saliency layout, edge layout). Strongest when the
  subjects differ (planet -> logo, wheel -> clock).
- **motion**: movement continues in the same direction and speed across the cut.
- **content**: similar subject from different footage (same object, new place).
- **colour**: palette rhyme only; weakest, use for transitions between scenes.

Craft:
- Line up the matched element precisely; reframe or scale B a few percent if needed.
- Cut A one or two frames after the frame shown; start B on its in frame.
- A sound bridge (B's audio under the end of A) or a whoosh/hit sells the jump.
- One or two per piece. If the idea does not connect, it is a gimmick.
