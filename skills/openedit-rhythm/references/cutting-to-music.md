# Cutting to music

## Reading the analysis
- `tempo` and `meter` give the beat and bar length (bar = 60 / BPM x meter seconds).
- `downbeats` are bar lines. Phrases are usually 4 or 8 bars; sections start on phrase lines.
- `sections[].kind`: intro, main, build (rising), drop (big energy jump), breakdown (energy falls),
  outro. `energy` is relative within the song.
- `ending.finalDownbeat` is the resolution; `tailEnd` is where the ring-out dies.

## Grids
- on-beat: every beat. Peak moments only; tiring over long stretches.
- every-2-beats: the default for energetic sections.
- downbeats: one cut per bar; most montages.
- phrase: every 4 bars; calm, scenic, documentary.

## Craft
- The cut on the drop is the most important cut of the piece: put the best shot there.
- In a build, tighten the cutting with the music (bar -> 2 beats -> beat), then release on the drop.
- After a fast passage, hold a shot for 2+ bars; contrast makes rhythm.
- A cut can land on a strong off-beat accent (a snare fill, a vocal hit) when the music does.
- Match motion across beat cuts (movement direction, screen position) so fast cutting stays readable.
- The last shot starts on the final downbeat and holds through the ring-out.
- Check alignment in review: aim for >= 90% of montage cuts within one frame of a beat.
